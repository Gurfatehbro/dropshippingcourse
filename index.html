Understanding The Opportunity

Why International Dropshipping Beats Domestic India Dropshipping

❌ Domestic Dropshipping in India (COD)
  • • 30%–45% RTO Disaster: Buyers reject parcels at the doorstep, charging you return courier penalties.
  • • Wafer-Thin Margins: Making barely ₹150–₹250 profit per item after ad spends.
  • • Cash Locked: COD courier remittances take 10–20 days to reach your account.
✅ International Dropshipping (Global USD)
  • • 100% Prepaid Orders: Foreign buyers pay upfront via Card/PayPal. Zero COD = 0% RTO!
  • • High-Ticket Profit: Earn $20 to $50 USD (₹1,600 to ₹4,000 INR) pure profit per single sale.
  • • Operate From India: Global suppliers air-ship directly to foreign customers in 7–10 days.
